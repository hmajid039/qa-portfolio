## 🐞 Description
In the iOS BynFor app, listings under Sales Referrals and Sales Referrals Commission display status values (Active / Deactive / Ordered) in the same black color, making them difficult to distinguish at a glance.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the BynFor iOS app(qacomet1) 2. Navigate to Sales Referrals 3. Open both Sales Referrals and Sales Referrals Commission sections 4. Observe the status text in listing entries


## ❌ Actual Result
All status values (Active, Deactive, Ordered) appear in the same black color and are not visually distinguishable.

## ✅ Expected Result
Status values should be displayed in distinct colors (and/or bold styling) to clearly differentiate each status.
