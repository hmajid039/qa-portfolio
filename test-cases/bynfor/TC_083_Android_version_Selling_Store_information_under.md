## 🐞 Description
On the Android version,in Selling Store information , under Privacy Settings, all checkboxes are pre-checked by default.
Users are unable to uncheck any of the options, even when attempting to change their privacy preferences. This prevents users from customizing their privacy settings and may lead to privacy concerns.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the application on an Android device . 2. Navigate to selling>Store information. 3. Observe that all checkboxes are pre-checked. 4. Attempt to uncheck any of the options. 5. Observe that the checkboxes cannot be unchecked.


## ❌ Actual Result
All checkboxes are pre-checked.

Users cannot uncheck or modify any privacy setting.

## ✅ Expected Result
Users should be able to freely check or uncheck privacy settings according to their preferences.

Pre-checked defaults are acceptable, but options must be editable.
