## 🐞 Description

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to the Registration page. 2. Hover over the Business button and note the tooltip position. 3. Hover over the Individual button and observe its tooltip position.


## ❌ Actual Result
Tooltip for the Individual button appears at the bottom and overlaps the Username field.

## ✅ Expected Result
Tooltip for the Individual button should appear properly aligned (on the right side), similar to the Business button, without overlapping any form fields.
