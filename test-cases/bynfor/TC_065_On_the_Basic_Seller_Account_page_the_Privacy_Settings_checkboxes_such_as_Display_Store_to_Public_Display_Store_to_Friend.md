## 🐞 Description
On the Basic Seller Account page, the Privacy Settings checkboxes, such as:" Display Store to Public, Display Store to Friends & Followers are disabled and unresponsive. The checkboxes are visually styled in blue but cannot be selected or changed, preventing sellers from updating their privacy preferences.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the application as a seller. 2. Navigate to the Basic Seller Account page. 3. Click on the Privacy Settings section. 4. Attempt to select or change the checkboxes for store visibility.


## ❌ Actual Result
All Privacy Settings checkboxes are disabled, unclickable, and visually highlighted in blue.

## ✅ Expected Result
All Privacy Settings checkboxes should be active and selectable, allowing sellers to update their store visibility preferences.
