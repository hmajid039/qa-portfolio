## 🐞 Description
After creating a Fine Art Gallery, the seller is able to add comments, outlook, and estimated price on their own Fine Art listing. These actions should typically be restricted to buyers or external viewers, not the listing owner.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to BynFor app and log in as a seller (Account: qacomet3) 2. Create a Fine Art Gallery 3. Create a Fine Art listing under the gallery 4. Open the listing as the same seller 5. Add a comment, outlook, and estimated price


## ❌ Actual Result
Seller is able to comment and submit outlook and estimated price on their own listing.

## ✅ Expected Result
Seller should not be allowed to comment or provide outlook/estimated price on their own Fine Art listing.
