## 🐞 Description
On the iOS app, the “Forgot Username/Password” popup displays confusing and inconsistent text. It says:

“Forgot your username? We will email it to the address we have on file.”

“Forgot your password? We can send a verification code to your email.”
The messaging feels inconsistent and not user-friendly, as both options should maintain a uniform tone and structure.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the iOS app. 2. Navigate to the Login page. 3. Tap on “Forgot Username/Password.” 4. Observe the text displayed in the popup.


## ❌ Actual Result
The popup shows inconsistent wording for username and password recovery, leading to a confusing user experience.

## ✅ Expected Result
The messages for “Forgot Username” and “Forgot Password” should be consistent in tone, format, and clarity.
Example:

“Forgot your username? We’ll email it to your registered address.”

“Forgot your password? We’ll send a verification code to your registered email.”
