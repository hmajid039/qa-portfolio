## 🐞 Description
On the Service Create Listing page, under the Service Information section, the title fields and corresponding text boxes are misaligned, making the form look cluttered and reducing readability.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to the Bynfor web application and log in. 2. Navigate to Profile → Listing → Create Listing → Service. 3. Scroll to the Service Information section. 4. Observe the alignment of the title labels and their corresponding text boxes.


## ❌ Actual Result
The title labels and text boxes are not properly aligned.

## ✅ Expected Result
The title labels and text boxes should be properly aligned in a consistent layout, ensuring a neat and readable form for users to enter service information.
