## 🐞 Description
In the desktop version of the application, when logged in with a Business Account, the “I don’t have SSN” checkbox is completely missing on the Store Information page.This prevents non SSN holders to create store as Birth Date box only appears when user click on "I don't have SSN checkbox"

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the desktop application using a Business Account. 2. Navigate to Selling → Store Information. 3. Locate the section for entering SSN details. 4. Check for the presence of the “I don’t have SSN” checkbox.


## ❌ Actual Result
The “I don’t have SSN” checkbox is completely missing for Business Accounts.

## ✅ Expected Result
The “I don’t have SSN” checkbox should be present.
