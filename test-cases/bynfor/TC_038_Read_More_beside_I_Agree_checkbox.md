## 🐞 Description
The “Read More”beside the “I Agree” checkbox contains multiple links (e.g., Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, etc.). However, clicking on any of these links redirects the user to the footer section of the page instead of opening their respective pages.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open Register page  2.Clcik on read more besides  “I Agree” checkbox. 2. Click on any of the links under “Read More” (e.g., Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy).


## ❌ Actual Result
Clicking on the links redirects to the footer section instead of opening the correct linked pages.

## ✅ Expected Result
Each “Read More” link should open its respective page (e.g., Terms & Conditions page, Privacy Policy page) instead of scrolling to the footer.
