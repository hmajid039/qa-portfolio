## 🐞 Description
After saving Store Information (for either Individual or Business accounts) and clicking the View Store button, the store rating is displayed using alternate asterisks (*) instead of proper star icons. This incorrect format reduces visual clarity and is inconsistent with expected rating UI.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the desktop application using an Individual or Business Account. 2. Click on Profile icon and Navigate to Selling → Store Information. 3. Fill in all required fields and click Apply and Save. 4. Once saved, You will see new Button on top of the page "view store".              5.click on the “View Store” button that appears. 6.Observe the rating display format on the View Store page.


## ❌ Actual Result
The store rating appears in the wrong format, displaying alternate asterisks (*) star icon tfor all.

## ✅ Expected Result
The store rating should display proper star icons (for example, ★ for filled stars and ☆ for empty stars) consistently for all rating positions.
