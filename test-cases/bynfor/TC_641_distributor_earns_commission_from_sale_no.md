## 🐞 Description
When a distributor earns a commission from a sale, no notification is sent when the commission first appears in the wallet with “Upcoming” status. Additionally, no notification is received when the order is completed and the commission status changes from “Upcoming” to “My Reward.” This makes it difficult for distributors to know when they have earned or received their commission

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Bynfor website as a distributor 2. Ensure the distributor application is approved and verification fee is paid 3. Share a product link as a distributor 4. Another user completes a purchase using the shared link 5. Check the wallet and notification panel 6. Wait for the order to complete and check notifications again


## ❌ Actual Result
1. No notification is received when the commission appears in the wallet as “Upcoming”
2. No notification is received when the commission moves from “Upcoming” to “My Reward”

## ✅ Expected Result
1. A notification should be sent when the commission is first added to the wallet as “Upcoming”
2. A notification should be sent when the order is completed and the commission becomes available under “My Reward”
