## 🐞 Description
When the Hamburger menu (“All”) is clicked, the categories listed under it are not properly aligned, making the menu look cluttered. Additionally, the last entry in the list is not visible even after scrolling through the menu.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the application/webpage. 2. Click on the Hamburger menu (“All”) in the header. 3. Observe the alignment of the categories under the menu. 4. Scroll to the bottom of the menu.


## ❌ Actual Result
1. Categories are misaligned under the Hamburger menu.
2. The last entry in the menu is not visible even after scrolling.

## ✅ Expected Result
All categories should be properly aligned in the menu.

The last entry should be fully visible when scrolling through the menu
