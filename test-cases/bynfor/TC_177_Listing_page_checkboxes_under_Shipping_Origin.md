## 🐞 Description
On the Listing page, the checkboxes under Shipping Origin and Shipping Insurance sections can be checked/unchecked by clicking on the label text next to them instead of only the checkbox itself.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1.Log in to the bynfor website. 2.Navigate to the Listing page. 3. Locate the Shipping Origin and Shipping Insurance sections. 4.Click on the text beside the checkbox (e.g., “Same as store address,” “Provide option to customers”).


## ❌ Actual Result
The checkbox gets checked or unchecked when clicking on the label text.

## ✅ Expected Result
The checkbox should be checkable only when clicking directly on the checkbox, not the adjacent text.
