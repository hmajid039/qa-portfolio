## 🐞 Description

1. Go to the Bynfor website https://www.bynfor.com/home                                2. Click on the “Login” button to open the popup.            3. Observe the alignment and size of the “Sign In” and “Register” buttons.


## ❌ Actual Result

## ✅ Expected Result
Both buttons should be of equal size and properly aligned within the popup.
