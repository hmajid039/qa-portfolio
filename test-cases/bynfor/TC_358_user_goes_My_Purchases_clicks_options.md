## 🐞 Description
When the user goes to My Purchases and clicks the options button on any purchased item, a side panel (left drawer) opens. However, the panel does not stay fixed on the left — it jumps to the center of the screen for a moment before repositioning back. This creates a visual flicker and disrupts user experience.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to Bynfor and Log in and click on the Profile icon. 2. Navigate to My Purchases. 3. Locate any purchased item. 4. Click on the options button. 5. Observe the side panel animation as it opens.


## ❌ Actual Result
The panel initially shifts to the center of the screen for about a second and then moves back to the left, causing a flicker effect.

## ✅ Expected Result
The panel should slide in smoothly from the left and remain fixed in its proper position without shifting.
