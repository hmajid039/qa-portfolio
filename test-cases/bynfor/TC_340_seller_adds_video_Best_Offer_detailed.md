## 🐞 Description
When a seller adds a video to a Best Offer detailed listing and clicks “Preview,” the preview shows “No video added” even though a video was uploaded. This issue persists across multiple preview attempts. Users are unable to verify that the video has been correctly attached, which can lead to incomplete or incorrect listings being published.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to Bynfor and Log in to the seller account(Account Used) 2. Create a Best Offer detailed listing and upload a video. 3. Click on the “Preview” button. 4. Observe the video section in the preview.


## ❌ Actual Result
The video section shows “No video added” even though a video was uploaded.

## ✅ Expected Result
The uploaded video should display correctly in the listing preview.
