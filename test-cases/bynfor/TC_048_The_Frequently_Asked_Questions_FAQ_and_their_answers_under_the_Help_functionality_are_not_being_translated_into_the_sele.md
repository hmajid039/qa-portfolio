## 🐞 Description
The Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) and their answers under the Help functionality are not being translated into the selected language.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the  "https://dev.bynfor.com/home"  and select a non-English language. 2. Navigate to the Help functionality on the header. 3. Check the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section. 4. Click on question links and check both questions and their answers.


## ❌ Actual Result
Both FAQ questions and answers remain in English despite selecting another language.

## ✅ Expected Result
All FAQ questions and answers should be displayed in the selected language.
