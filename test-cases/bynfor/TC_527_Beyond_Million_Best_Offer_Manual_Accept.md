## 🐞 Description
In Beyond Million Best Offer (Manual Accept Offer), the Reserve Price field is correctly hidden on Dev BynFor, but on Admin the field shows as empty and triggers mandatory validation even though Reserve Price is not required for manual approval.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to BynFor app and log in (Account: qacomet3) 2. Create a Beyond Million → Best Offer listing with Manual Accept Offer 3. Check that Reserve Price is not shown on Dev 4. Open the listing on Admin side 5. Try to approve the listing


## ❌ Actual Result
Admin is forced to enter a Reserve Price due to mandatory validation while updating listing..

## ✅ Expected Result
Reserve Price should not be mandatory for Manual Accept Offer
