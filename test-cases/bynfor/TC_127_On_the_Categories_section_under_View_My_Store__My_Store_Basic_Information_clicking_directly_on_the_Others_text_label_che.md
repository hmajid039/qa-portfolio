## 🐞 Description
On the Categories section under View My Store → My Store Basic Information, clicking directly on the “Others” text label checks or unchecks the checkbox.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Login to website.     2. Navigate to SELLER CENTER from sidebar.  3. Click on Get a Plan. 4. Click on View my store button.   5. My Store Basic Information. page will open.  6. In the Categories section click on the “Others” text label (not the checkbox).


## ❌ Actual Result
Clicking the “Others” text toggles the checkbox state.

## ✅ Expected Result
Only clicking directly on the checkbox should check or uncheck it. The label text should remain non-interactive.
