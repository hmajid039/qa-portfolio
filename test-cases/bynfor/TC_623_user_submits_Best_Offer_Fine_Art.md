## 🐞 Description
When a user submits a Best Offer on a Fine Art listing, the system fails to process the request and displays a “Something went wrong on server” error, preventing successful offer submission.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the BynFor app and log in 2. Navigate to Fine Art and open any exhibition listing 3. Click on Best Offer 4. Enter a valid offer amount 5. Submit the offer


## ❌ Actual Result
System shows “Something went wrong on server” error and the offer is not submitted.

## ✅ Expected Result
Best Offer should be submitted successfully and reflected in the offer history.
