## 🐞 Description
On the Seller My Store account page, the SSN Number field is marked with an asterisk (*) indicating it is mandatory for all account types. However, the SSN number should only be required for Personal accounts, not for Business accounts.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the application as a seller. on personal account 2. Navigate to the My Store account page and check SSN where it is clearly mention SSN is mandatory if it is personal account. 3. Now Logged In on  Business account  4. Observe the SSN Number field with asterisk.


## ❌ Actual Result
The SSN Number field is marked with a red asterisk (*) as mandatory even for Business accounts.

## ✅ Expected Result
The SSN Number field should only be mandatory for Personal accounts and should not display an asterisk (*) or validation requirement for Business accounts.
