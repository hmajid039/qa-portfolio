## 🐞 Description
When creating or editing a listing from a business account in the BynFor Android app, the app performance becomes very slow. The process does not complete, the app navigates back automatically, stops responding, or shows a system pop-up asking to close the app.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Login to BynFor Android app using a business account 2. Go to create a new listing or edit an existing listing 3. Enter listing details 4. Try to save or proceed


## ❌ Actual Result
The app works slowly, fails to complete the process, goes back automatically, freezes, or shows a “App not responding / Close app” pop-up.

## ✅ Expected Result
The app should work smoothly while creating or editing a listing, complete the process successfully, and should not freeze, crash, or show any system error pop-up.
