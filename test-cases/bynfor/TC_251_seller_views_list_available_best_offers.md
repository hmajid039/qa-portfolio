## 🐞 Description
When a seller views the list of available best offers from their own account, their own published listings are also visible. Upon clicking their listing, a “Make Offer” button appears, allowing them to attempt to make an offer on their own product. Although this button disappears after some time, it should never be displayed to the listing owner.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce


## ❌ Actual Result
The seller can see the “Make Offer” button on their own listing for a short time before it disappears automatically.

## ✅ Expected Result
The “Make Offer” button should never appear on listings created by the same seller account.
