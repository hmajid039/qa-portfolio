## 🐞 Description
In the Fine Art listing section of BynFor, the serial numbers assigned to listings are incorrect (misordered/duplicated/skipped), causing confusion when identifying or referencing specific listings.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the BynFor app and log in 2. Navigate to Fine Art 3. View the list of Fine Art listings 4. Observe the serial numbers displayed for each listing


## ❌ Actual Result
Serial numbers are not displayed correctly and do not follow the expected order

## ✅ Expected Result
Serial numbers should be displayed correctly in a proper sequential order for all Fine Art listings.
