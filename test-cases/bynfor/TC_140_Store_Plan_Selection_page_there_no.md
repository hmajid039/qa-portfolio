## 🐞 Description
On the Store Plan Selection page, there is no visible separation line between the plan name (e.g., Bronze) and its feature details.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Login to the bunfor app from your mobile.  2. Go to SELLER CENTER from sidebar. 3. Click on Create Profile.  4. Scroll down and click on Purchase Store Plan . 5. Click on down any purchase plan (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and VIP) dropdown


## ❌ Actual Result
There is no separation line between the plan name and details, making the content appear merged and less structured.

## ✅ Expected Result
A clear separation line or visual divider should be present between the plan name and its details for better readability and consistency with the web version.
