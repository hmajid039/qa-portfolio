## 🐞 Description
On the iOS app, when a seller creates a listing with a Best Offer option and enters a specific offer price, the final listed price displayed after submission is not accurate. The system shows a different amount than the one entered by the seller during listing creation

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the iOS app. 2. Log in with a Seller Account. 3. Go to the Create Listing page and create a Best Offer listing and enter a specific offer price (e.g., $50). 4.Complete the listing and save. 5. Check the listing details after it goes live.


## ❌ Actual Result
The listed price shown under the Best Offer section is different from the value entered during listing setup.

## ✅ Expected Result
The price displayed in the live listing should exactly match the Best Offer price entered by the seller during listing creation.
