1. Open the iOS app. 2. Navigate to the Business Registration page. 3. Scroll through the form fields. 4. Observe that the “Insurance Company” checkbox and “EIN” textbox are not present.

“Insurance Company” checkbox and “EIN” textbox are missing from the Business Registration form.

## ✅ Expected Result
Both “Insurance Company” checkbox and “EIN” textbox should be displayed on the Business Registration page, similar to other platforms.
