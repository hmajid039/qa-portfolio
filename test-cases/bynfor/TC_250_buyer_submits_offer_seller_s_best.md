## 🐞 Description
When a buyer submits an offer on a seller’s best offer listing, the seller does not receive any notification (push, in-app, or email). This prevents the seller from knowing that a new offer has been made, potentially delaying responses or causing missed deals.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce


## ❌ Actual Result
No notification is received by the seller when a buyer makes an offer.

## ✅ Expected Result
The seller should receive a notification (push and/or in-app) immediately when a buyer makes an offer on their best offer listing.
