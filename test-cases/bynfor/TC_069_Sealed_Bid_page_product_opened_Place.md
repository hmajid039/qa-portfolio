## 🐞 Description
On the Sealed Bid page, when a product is opened , the Place Bid button is visible on the right side of the page but does not perform any action upon clicking. No popup or redirection occurs.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to the Sealed Bid section from the top navigation.            2. Open the listed product  3. Click the Place Bid button on the product detail page.


## ❌ Actual Result
Clicking the Place Bid button does not trigger any response, no popup or redirection.

## ✅ Expected Result
Clicking the Place Bid button should open the bid popup or redirect to the bidding form page where the user can enter their bid amount.
