## 🐞 Description
On the payment page, the checkboxes under “Use Wallet Funds” (Skill Reward, Earnings, Monetary Gifts) are visible but not clickable.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to the website.   2. After login go to Instant Buy.   3. Click on a product you want to buy.   4. Click Buy Now.  5. On the payment page, try to select one or more checkboxes under "Use Wallet Funds".


## ❌ Actual Result
Checkboxes are not responsive and cannot be selected or deselected

## ✅ Expected Result
The user should be able to select/deselect multiple wallet fund options
