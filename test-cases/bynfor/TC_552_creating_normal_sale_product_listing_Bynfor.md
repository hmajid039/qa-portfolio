## 🐞 Description
When creating a normal sale product listing in the Bynfor Android app, selecting the USPS shipping option causes the app to crash. The crash happens right after the USPS shipping popup opens, and any data already entered in the listing form is lost

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open Bynfor Android app (majidbusiness / Sun@Ray123) 2. Go to Seller Area → My Listing → Create Listing 3. Choose Normal Sale Listing 4. Fill in some product details (e.g. title, price, category, etc.). 5. Scroll to the Shipping section 6. Tap on USPS Shipping 7. Wait for the USPS shipping details popup to open and observe


## ❌ Actual Result
The USPS shipping popup appears briefly and then the app crashes and user is taken out of the app
Any entered listing details are lost

## ✅ Expected Result
The USPS shipping popup should open and remain stable. User should be able to enter or select USPS shipping details without the app crashing and previously entered listing details should remain intact
