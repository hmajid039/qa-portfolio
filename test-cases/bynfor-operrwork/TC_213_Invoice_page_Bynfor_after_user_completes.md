## 🐞 Description
On the Invoice page in Bynfor, after the user completes a payment of $31, the generated invoice incorrectly shows the paid amount as $15. This creates confusion for users and may cause discrepancies in payment records and financial reporting

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Bynfor platform. 2. Complete a transaction or purchase totaling $31. 3. Navigate to the Invoice section to view the generated invoice. 4. Observe the displayed amount on the invoice.


## ❌ Actual Result
The invoice shows $15 as the paid amount, even though the user has paid $31.

## ✅ Expected Result
The invoice should correctly display the actual paid amount ($31) that matches the transaction total.
