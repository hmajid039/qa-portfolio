## 🐞 Description
In the Store Layout → Templates dropdown under the Settings tab, the text for options containing letters with descenders (like “g”, “p”, “q”, “y”) is being truncated at the bottom. This affects readability and makes the dropdown look visually inconsistent.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1.Log in to Bynfor website. 2. Navigate to Seller page from sidebar. 3.Go to the Settings tab. 4.Open the Templates dropdown under Store Layout. 5.Select “Food and Beverage” or “Real Estate Template” and observe the text in the field.


## ❌ Actual Result
Letters with descenders (e.g., “g”, “p”, “q”, “y”) are partially cut off at the bottom in the dropdown list.

## ✅ Expected Result
All text in the dropdown should be fully visible, with proper spacing so that no part of any letter is truncated.
