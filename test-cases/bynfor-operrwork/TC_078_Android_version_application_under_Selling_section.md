## 🐞 Description
On the Android version of the application, under the Selling section in the Store, the Upload and Delete icons for product images are displayed at the bottom of the image container.
On the Desktop version, these icons appear on the right side of the image, which is more intuitive.
Displaying the icons at the bottom on Android looks unnecessary and affects user experience and UI consistency across platforms.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the application on an Android device. 2. Navigate to Selling → Store. 3. Upload or view an existing product image. 4. Observe that the Upload and Delete icons appear at the bottom of the image container. 5. Compare with the Desktop version — icons are on the right side.


## ❌ Actual Result
Upload and Delete icons are positioned at the bottom of the image container.

This placement looks unnecessary and is inconsistent with Desktop UI.

## ✅ Expected Result
Upload and Delete icons should be positioned on the right side of the image container, similar to the Desktop version, for consistent UI and better usability.
