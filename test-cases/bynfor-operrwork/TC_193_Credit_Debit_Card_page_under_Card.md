## 🐞 Description
On the Credit/Debit Card page, under the Card Billing Address section, the checkbox labeled "Same as store address" behaves incorrectly.  It can be toggled by clicking:            1.The checkbox itself
2.The label text
3.The white space horizontally next to the label
4.And even two small white boxes that appear vertically while toggling the checkbox.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1.Navigate to the Credit/Debit Card page. 2.Scroll down to the Card Billing Address section. 3.Click on the "Same as store address" text. 4.Click on the horizontal white space next to the text. 5.Observe two small white boxes appearing vertically when toggling. 6.Click on those boxes ,notice the checkbox toggles as well..


## ❌ Actual Result
The checkbox can be toggled by clicking on multiple unintended areas like text, white space, and small white boxes.

## ✅ Expected Result
The checkbox should toggle only when clicking on the checkbox itself (or the label text if designed that way). No other UI element should trigger this action.
