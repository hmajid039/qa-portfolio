## 🐞 Description
In the Android version of the application, when logged in with a business account, the account/profile name is not displayed in the top-right corner of the screen after clicking the account icon (located at the upper right corner above the “Summary” tab). This issue prevents users from identifying which business account they are currently logged into.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the application on an Android device. 2. Log in using a business account. 3. Click on the Profile icon 4. Observe the above Summary missing the profile/account name.


## ❌ Actual Result
No account name or profile name is displayed for the business account.

## ✅ Expected Result
The business account name/profile name should be visible in the top-right section when clicked above the Summary tab, similar to personal accounts.
