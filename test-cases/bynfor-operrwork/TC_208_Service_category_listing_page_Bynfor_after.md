## 🐞 Description
On the Service category listing page in Bynfor, after uploading a Business Logo that meets the required dimensions, the image preview area continues to display the default placeholder instead of the uploaded image.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Bynfor platform. 2. Click on the Profile icon and select Listing. 3. Select the Service category. 4. Upload a Business Logo image that meets the specified dimension requirements. 5. Observe the preview section after upload.


## ❌ Actual Result
The image preview section continues to show the placeholder instead of displaying the uploaded logo.

## ✅ Expected Result
After uploading a valid logo image, the preview section should display the uploaded image instead of the placeholder.
