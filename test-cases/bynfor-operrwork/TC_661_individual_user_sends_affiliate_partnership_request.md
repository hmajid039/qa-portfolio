## 🐞 Description
When an individual user sends an affiliate partnership request to a business account, the request is not displayed in the business account dashboard, preventing the business from accepting or rejecting the request.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in with an individual account. 2. Send affiliate partnership request to a business account. 3. Log out. 4. Log in with the respective business account. 5. Navigate to Affiliate/Partnership Requests section.


## ❌ Actual Result
No affiliate partnership request is shown in the business account.

## ✅ Expected Result
The affiliate partnership request should appear in the business account so it can be reviewed and accepted or rejected.
