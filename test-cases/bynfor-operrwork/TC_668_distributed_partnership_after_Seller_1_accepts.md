## 🐞 Description
In distributed partnership, after Seller 1 accepts a distributor request and distributor sells Seller 1’s product, Seller 1’s own account shows a Commission tab with a store dropdown containing Seller 1’s name, which is invalid.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Login to Bynfor dev(qacomet3)             2. Go to partnership programme and  accepts distributor partnership request 3. Distributor sells Seller  product 4. Log in to Seller  account 5. Open Commission tab and check store dropdown


## ❌ Actual Result
Commission tab is visible and dropdown shows Seller 1’s own name as a selectable store.

## ✅ Expected Result
Seller account should not show Commission tab or self-name in store dropdown; commission view should be limited to distributor/recruiter roles only.
