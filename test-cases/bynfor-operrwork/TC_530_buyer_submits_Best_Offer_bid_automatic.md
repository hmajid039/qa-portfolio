## 🐞 Description
When a buyer submits a Best Offer bid on an automatic listing, the first bid works correctly and the product is bought. However, if the buyer places another bid on the same product, the bid is accepted but the Pay button is disabled, preventing purchase.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in as a buyer on BynFor 2. Place a Best Offer bid on an automatic listing and complete the purchase 3. Place another bid on the same product 4. Observe the Pay button


## ❌ Actual Result
Bid is accepted but the Pay button is disabled, so the buyer cannot complete purchase.

## ✅ Expected Result
The Pay button should be enabled for repeated bids, allowing the buyer to complete the purchase.
