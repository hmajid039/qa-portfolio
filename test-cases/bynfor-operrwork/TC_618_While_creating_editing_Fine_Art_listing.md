## 🐞 Description
While creating or editing a Fine Art listing in BynFor, the system displays a “Video URL is not valid” error even when a valid video URL is entered, preventing users from adding videos to their listings.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open BynFor app and log in 2. Navigate to Fine Art → Create/Edit Listing 3. Enter a valid video URL in the Video URL field 4. Submit or move focus away from the field


## ❌ Actual Result
System shows “Video URL is not valid” error and does not accept the URL.

## ✅ Expected Result
Valid video URLs should be accepted and successfully attached to the Fine Art listing.
