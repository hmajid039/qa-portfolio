## 🐞 Description
When a user clicks the Clear Cart button on the Cart page, the items remain visible even after waiting 10–12 seconds. Navigating away from the cart page and returning back shows the cart as empty

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Bynfor app android(appuser / Sun@Ray123) 2. Navigate to cart 3. Click on Clear Cart (top right corner) 4. Wait 10–12 seconds. Observe that items are still displayed 5. Navigate away to Home page, then click the Cart icon 6. Observe that the cart now shows empty


## ❌ Actual Result
Clicking Clear Cart does not show visible remove functionality and no feedback shown

## ✅ Expected Result
Clicking Clear Cart should immediately remove all items or show a loading indicator until the action completes. Cart should display consistent information across pages
