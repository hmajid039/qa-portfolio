## 🐞 Description
On the Store Plan selection page located under the Selling section, the “Annual” button is unresponsive.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1.Go to the Bynfor web application and log in. 2. Click on the Profile icon and navigate to the Selling section. 3. Go to Become a Seller, then scroll down until you find the Purchase Store Plan option and click it. 4. Locate the Annual button at the top of the page. 5. Attempt to click on the Annual button.


## ❌ Actual Result
The “Annual” button is not clickable — it does not respond or change the selection when clicked.

## ✅ Expected Result
The “Annual” button should be clickable and allow users to select the annual plan option.
