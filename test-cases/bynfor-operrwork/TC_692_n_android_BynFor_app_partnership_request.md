## 🐞 Description
n the android BynFor app, When a partnership request is sent to a Business Account, the system does not clearly distinguish between newly received (pending) partnership requests and already approved requests. This makes it difficult for business users to identify which requests require action.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the BynFor app as an Individual Account. 2. Send a partnership request to a Business Account. 3. Log in to the Business Account. 4. Navigate to the partnership requests section. 5. Review the list of partnership requests.


## ❌ Actual Result
The Business Account displays partnership requests without any clear differentiation between pending (upcoming) requests and already approved requests. All requests appear similar in the list.

## ✅ Expected Result
The Business Account should clearly differentiate partnership requests by status (e.g.,highlighted) so that upcoming requests are easily identifiable and actionable.
