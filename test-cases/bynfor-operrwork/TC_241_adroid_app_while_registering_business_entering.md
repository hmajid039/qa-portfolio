## 🐞 Description
On the adroid app while registering a business, when entering text in the “Business Official Name” and “Store Name” fields, pressing the space bar not only prevents entering a space but also deletes the previously typed text.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the android app. 2. Navigate to the Business Registration page. 3. Try entering a multi-word name (e.g., ABC Traders or Modern Fashion Store) in the “Business Official Name” or “Store Name” field. 4. Observe the behavior.


## ❌ Actual Result
The space is not inserted, and the previously typed text is deleted.

## ✅ Expected Result
The fields should allow spaces between words
