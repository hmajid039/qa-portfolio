## 🐞 Description
On the Active Listings page in Bynfor, after a user successfully lists a product, the product card is displayed correctly. However, when the user clicks on the listing card, the card layout gets squeezed or compressed from the lower part

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to the Bynfor platform. 2. Successfully create and publish a product listing. 3. Navigate to the Active Listings section. 4. Click on any product card from the active listings.


## ❌ Actual Result
When the listing card is clicked, it gets squeezed or compressed from the bottom, distorting the card’s appearance.

## ✅ Expected Result
Clicking on the listing card should not alter its layout. The card should maintain consistent spacing and dimensions.
