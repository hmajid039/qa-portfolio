## 🐞 Description
On the Sealed Bid page, when the user clicks the “Price Estimation” link, a popup appears asking to verify that the user is above 18 years of age. However, after selecting the birth year from the dropdown menu, the selected year does not appear in the field.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to the Sealed Bid page.         2. Click on the Price Estimation link. 3. In the popup, click to select your Birth Year from the dropdown.  4. Observe that the selected year does not populate in the field.


## ❌ Actual Result
The selected birth year from the dropdown does not appear in the field.

## ✅ Expected Result
The selected birth year should appear correctly in the field after selection.
