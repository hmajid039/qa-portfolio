## 🐞 Description
In the admin Gallery Event listing, the Privacy Policy is displayed as a clickable link. However, on the iOS app the same Privacy Policy appears only as plain text and is not clickable.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to BynFor admin and add a Privacy Policy link to a Gallery Event 2. Open the same Gallery Event on the iOS app 3. Observe the Privacy Policy field


## ❌ Actual Result
Privacy Policy is displayed as plain text on iOS and cannot be clicked.

## ✅ Expected Result
Privacy Policy should be displayed as a clickable link on iOS, same as admin/web.
