## 🐞 Description
In the Real Estate listing section, the “Store Plan Information” and the “Change Store Plan” option are missing. These options are essential for sellers to view their current subscription plan and switch to another plan if needed. The absence of these options limits sellers from managing their store plan effectively within the Real Estate module.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to BynFor app and log in (Account: qacomet3) 2. Navigate to the “Real Estate” category 3. Start creating or editing a Real Estate listing 4. Scroll to where “Store Plan Information” and “Change Store Plan” should appear


## ❌ Actual Result
Both the “Store Plan Information” and the “Change Store Plan” option are missing under the Real Estate listing section.

## ✅ Expected Result
The “Store Plan Information” section and a functional “Change Store Plan” option should be visible under the Real Estate listing just like web, allowing sellers to view or update their store plan.
