## 🐞 Description
On the Business Registration page, when selecting “Insurance Company” and opening the “Types of Insurance” dropdown, clicking on the text label next to a checkbox also checks or unchecks it. This behavior should only occur when the checkbox itself is clicked.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the bynfor website.                                2.Click on Register 3. Select Business account type. 4. Check the Insurance Company option. 5. Click on any text label under Types of Insurance dropdown.


## ❌ Actual Result
Clicking the text label toggles the checkbox selection.

## ✅ Expected Result
Only clicking directly on the checkbox should toggle the selection, clicking the text should not change the checkbox state.
