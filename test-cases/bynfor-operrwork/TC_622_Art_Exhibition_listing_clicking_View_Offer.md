## 🐞 Description
In the Art Exhibition listing, clicking “View Offer History” opens a blank white page when there are no offers available. This provides no feedback to the user and appears as a broken screen instead of an empty-state message.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the BynFor app and log in 2. Navigate to Art Exhibition 3. Open a listing with no offer history 4. Click on View Offer History


## ❌ Actual Result
A blank white page is displayed with no message or UI.

## ✅ Expected Result
A clear empty-state message such as “No offers found” should be displayed.
