## 🐞 Description
When a buyer places a bid of 888,889 on a Beyond Million Best Offer listing and the bid is accepted, the final price charged becomes 888,888.10, even though there is no discount applied.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in as a buyer on BynFor(qacomet1) 2. Place a bid of 888,889 on a Beyond Million Best Offer listing 3. Wait for the bid to be accepted 4. Observe the final purchase price


## ❌ Actual Result
Buyer is charged 888,888.10, which is lower than the bid amount.

## ✅ Expected Result
Buyer should be charged the exact bid amount (888,889) since no discount is applied.
