## 🐞 Description
The product names displayed under the Instant Buy listing are not being translated when a non-English language is selected.

## 🔁 Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the  "https://dev.bynfor.com/home" and select any non-English language. 2. Navigate to the Instant Buy section. 3. Observe the product names listed


## ❌ Actual Result
Product names in the Instant Buy listing are displayed in English only, regardless of the selected language.

## ✅ Expected Result
All product names in the Instant Buy listing should translate according to the selected language.
